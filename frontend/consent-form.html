1. Invitation

You are invited to participate in a research study conducted by the Centre for Educational Technology, Indian Institute of Technology Bombay. Before deciding whether to participate, please read this information carefully. If anything is unclear, you are encouraged to ask questions. Your participation is entirely voluntary.

2. Purpose of the Study

The purpose of this study is to evaluate the usability of ML-Tutor, a computer-based learning environment. The study aims to understand how learners interact with the system and identify opportunities to improve its design, usability, accessibility, and overall learning experience.

The findings from this research will contribute towards the design of future intelligent tutoring systems and adaptive educational technologies.

3. Why Have I Been Invited?

You have been invited because you are an adult participant (18 years or older) who is able to complete computer-based learning activities.

Participation is not based on academic performance, prior experience, gender, or background.

4. What Will I Be Asked To Do?

Participation involves a single session lasting approximately 60–90 minutes.

During the study you may be asked to:

  • Complete a short demographic questionnaire.
  • Use ML-Tutor to complete one or more learning activities.
  • Answer brief questionnaires before, during, or after the activity.
  • Provide feedback regarding your learning experience.

The following information may also be collected during the study:

  • Mouse movements and clicks
  • Keyboard interactions
  • Navigation behaviour
  • Time taken to complete learning tasks
  • Learning performance
  • Webcam-based affect estimation

No webcam images or video recordings will be stored. The webcam is used only for estimating affective states during the learning session.

5. Risks and Benefits

This study involves minimal risk. Some participants may experience temporary fatigue, frustration, or mild discomfort while interacting with the learning environment. You may request a break at any time or discontinue participation without any penalty.

Although there may be no direct personal benefit, your participation will contribute to improving educational technologies for future learners.

6. Privacy and Confidentiality

Your privacy is important to us.

  • Your identity will be replaced with a unique participant identification number.
  • Your name will never appear in research publications.
  • Only authorised members of the research team will have access to identifiable information.
  • Research data will be stored securely on password-protected systems.
  • Only anonymised or aggregated data will be reported.

7. Voluntary Participation

Participation is completely voluntary. You may:

  • Decline to participate.
  • Skip any question.
  • Withdraw from the study at any time.
  • Stop participation without giving any reason.

There will be no penalty or loss of benefits for choosing not to participate.

8. Data Storage

Anonymised research data may be retained for future academic research in accordance with IIT Bombay's research data policies.
